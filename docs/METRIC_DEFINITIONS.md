# Metric Definitions & Standard KPI Formulas
**Project:** Frogeindrip Analytics Platform  
**Governance:** Unified metric definition layer shared across SQL, dbt, Python, Power BI, and AI Copilot  

---

## 1. Commercial & Financial Metrics

### Gross Sales
- **Definition:** The total retail value of all items sold before deducting discounts, returns, or allowances.
- **Grain:** Order item or order level.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Gross Sales} = \sum (\text{Quantity} \times \text{Unit Price})$$
- **Filters:** Only completed settled orders (`status = 'completed'`).

### Promotional Discounts
- **Definition:** Price reductions granted via checkout discount codes or automated promotions.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Discounts} = \sum \text{discount\_amount}$$

### Net Sales
- **Definition:** Total revenue earned after deducting promotional discounts. Represents the true operational top-line.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Net Sales} = \text{Gross Sales} - \text{Discounts}$$
- **Square Screenshot Reference:** Net Sales in Group A = **$46,271.26**.

### Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- **Definition:** The direct manufacturing and procurement cost of garments sold.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{COGS} = \sum (\text{Quantity} \times \text{Unit Cost})$$

### Gross Profit
- **Definition:** Realized contribution profit before operating expenses (marketing, payroll, software).
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Net Sales} - \text{COGS}$$

### Gross Margin %
- **Definition:** Gross profit expressed as a percentage of net sales.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Gross Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Net Sales}} \times 100$$

### Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Definition:** The average net revenue generated per completed order.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{AOV} = \frac{\text{Total Net Sales}}{\text{Total Completed Orders}}$$

## 2. Customer & Retention Metrics

### Repeat Purchase Rate
- **Definition:** The proportion of total purchasing customers who placed more than one completed order.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Repeat Purchase Rate} = \frac{\text{Count of Customers with } \ge 2 \text{ Orders}}{\text{Total Unique Customers}} \times 100$$

### Repeat Buyer GMV Share (Method A: Lifetime Cohort)
- **Definition:** Total net sales generated by all customers who ever placed $\ge 2$ orders in their lifetime.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Repeat GMV Share (Lifetime)} = \frac{\sum_{\text{Cust } \in \text{ Repeaters}} \text{Lifetime Net Spend}}{\text{Total Platform GMV}} \times 100$$
- **Note:** Useful for total retrospective customer value audits; contains lookahead bias for early calendar months.

### Repeat Buyer GMV Share (Method B: Point-in-Time Sequence)
- **Definition:** Net sales generated strictly on order sequences $\ge 2$ at the time the transaction occurred.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Repeat GMV Share (PIT)} = \frac{\sum \text{Net Amount where } \text{order\_seq} \ge 2}{\text{Total Platform GMV}} \times 100$$
- **Note:** Non-leaking, mathematically strict financial accounting standard.

### Inter-Purchase Interval (Days to Repeat)
- **Definition:** Number of elapsed days between a customer's first order and their second order.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Days Between Orders} = \text{DATE\_DIFF}(\text{Order 2 Date}, \text{Order 1 Date})$$

## 3. Growth & Time-Intelligence Metrics

### Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth %
- **Definition:** Percentage change in net sales compared to the immediate prior calendar month.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{MoM Growth \%} = \frac{\text{Net Sales}_t - \text{Net Sales}_{t-1}}{\text{Net Sales}_{t-1}} \times 100$$
- **Boundary Rule:** When $\text{Net Sales}_{t-1} = 0$, MoM growth is mathematically undefined and recorded as `NULL` or labeled as "Initial Launch Baseline".
